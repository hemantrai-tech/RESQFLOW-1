06 A careful path from message to decision
01
Intake · Many formatsReceive

Let every signal arrive.

The broader product brief covers voice, text and image reports in English, Hindi-English and Tamil-English. The repository’s current local exercise accepts text and keeps the original report attached to the extracted fields.

Voice · plannedText · demo-readyImage · planned
02
Understanding · Structured fieldsExtract

Find what matters in the message.

Agents identify the reported need, place, people affected, danger cues, language and details still missing. Unknown stays unknown until someone can verify it.

Need · floodPlace · Block BPeople · needs confirmation
03
Deduplication · Time + place + typeCluster

Bring related reports together.

Reports about the same event become one developing incident. Relatedness uses location, time and event type, while source reports remain attached as evidence.

04
Evidence · Support + conflictCompare

Keep disagreement visible.

Confirming reports, conflicting reports and gaps are shown together. A report that disagrees with the group is flagged for review, not thrown away.

Supporting reportsConflicts remain visibleMissing details surfaced
05
Triage · Deterministic rulesExplain

Make the priority legible.

Code applies the configured triage factors. The same incident state produces the same score; the language model does not choose the priority.

People at risk +Evidence +Conflict checked
06
Action planner · Human approvalDecide

Wait for the person in charge.

A resource can be recommended, but no task is created until an operator deliberately approves. The flood exercise records a simulated action only.

01 / Understand
◎

Never invent the missing piece

Unknown location, headcount or floor remains unknown and is surfaced as a question for the operator.

02 / Preserve uncertainty
≋

Evidence stays attached

Every incident carries its source reports, supporting signals, conflicts and timeline into review.

03 / Keep provenance
⌁

People stay responsible

AI can assist with extraction. Deterministic code sets priority. A human reviews every simulated action.

04 / Human approval
A recommendation is not a dispatch

A human stays
in control.

ResQFlow can suggest the nearest suitable unit in the exercise data. It cannot contact a real responder. The operator reviews the evidence first, then chooses what to do.
